# Community conduct

In this repository's issues, pull requests, and project discussions, communicate respectfully, focus criticism on the work, and respect others' privacy. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

A conduct reporting contact and an enforcement owner have not yet been designated. Until they are established, this document states expected behavior but does not promise a private reporting channel, response deadline, or enforcement process. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

See [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md) for contribution guidance.

## Working together

Give people room to ask questions and correct mistakes. Explain disagreements with concrete examples, avoid personal attacks, and get permission before sharing another person's private messages or identifying details. English and Japanese contributions are welcome.

## 日本語

Issue、PR、プロジェクト内の議論では、相手を尊重し、指摘は人ではなく内容に向けてください。質問や訂正を受け入れ、意見が異なる場合は具体例で説明してください。嫌がらせ、脅迫、差別的な発言、個人情報の公開は認めません。

相談窓口と対応責任者は未確定です。この文書は行動指針を示すもので、非公開の相談経路や対応期限を保証するものではありません。機微な相談内容を公開しないでください。
